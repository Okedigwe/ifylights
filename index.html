About Ify Lights Stage and Studio

At Ify Lights Stage and Studio, we believe lighting is more than visibility — it is the heartbeat of every great performance. From our base in Lagos we supply professional-grade stage and studio lighting that turns a simple stage into an immersive experience.

Whether you are lighting a church auditorium, a wedding or event centre, a club, a concert or a TV and photo studio, we stock the fixtures, controllers, effects and rigging to make your vision shine.

  • Industry expertise We understand the technical demands of live events.

  • Versatility Equipment for everything from nightclubs to professional studios.
